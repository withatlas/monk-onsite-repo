# FDE Onsite Prompt

You are joining a customer call for a SaaS company that wants to reduce manual cash application work. They have invoices in the system and bank transactions coming from their operating account exports.

Use the existing product and codebase to form a recommendation and choose a focused build.

During the initial analysis period, do not use AI tools. After you present your analysis and align with your interviewer on a scoped build, you may use AI tools during implementation. Be ready to explain your reasoning, tradeoffs, and code.

For FDE, bias toward customer understanding, recommendations, rollout plan, operational scalability, and a scoped implementation that improves the workflow.

Some customer payments include messy remittance context. In your recommendation and build, consider where model-assisted matching belongs, how to keep it safe, and when a finance user should review instead of auto-apply.

In the discussion portion, be ready to cover:

- what you would recommend to the customer
- what you would recommend internally
- how you would sequence the work
- what you would build first and why
- how deterministic matching, model-assisted matching, and human review should work together

Then choose a scoped improvement and build it. Optimize for clear tradeoffs, customer impact, and a change that could realistically ship.
