# FDE Onsite Prompt

You are joining a customer call for a SaaS company that wants to reduce manual cash application work. They have invoices in the system and bank transactions coming from their operating account.

Spend the first 30 minutes understanding the current system and forming a plan. Do not use AI during that first analysis window.

Your analysis should include:

- what the system currently does
- where the current workflow breaks down for the customer
- what you would recommend to the customer
- what you would recommend internally
- a Phase 1 / Phase 2 / Phase 3 roadmap

After the analysis window, choose a scoped improvement and build it. Optimize for clear tradeoffs, customer impact, and a change that could realistically ship.

You may use AI during the build portion after you have presented your analysis.
